Review of estimated credit-score explanations

Reason-integrity review for an estimated external credit score
Historical record: 8193

PREDICTIONS AND EXPLANATIONS
The Random Forest predicts 95.5% serious-late-payment risk with the supplied score, 95.3% with the Ridge estimate and 95.5% with the gradient-boosting estimate. The leading individual-factor sets differ between the two estimates. The grouped-factor sets match.

EVIDENCE FROM THE RANDOM FOREST
The external score contributes to the study’s Random Forest prediction relative to 24 reference records.

EVIDENCE FROM THE SCORE ESTIMATORS
The reconstructed-system explanations describe the Random Forest using a score estimated by Ridge regression or gradient-boosted trees.

WHAT REMAINS UNESTABLISHED
The inferred credit factors were used to calculate the original external credit-risk score.

POLICY RECOMMENDATION
If a consumer-facing reason depends on a material external score, separate what the lender model establishes from what can be supported about the score provider. A reconstruction is useful for testing, but provider-linked reasons or validation records are still needed for claims about the upstream score.

CONSUMER-ADVOCATE TAKEAWAY
An external provider’s opacity should not leave the applicant with a specific reason that the creditor cannot connect to the decision path actually used.

RECORDS NEEDED FOR FURTHER REVIEW
Obtain applicable provider reason codes, score/model version, input documentation and validation evidence linking proposed reasons to the original score. For a refusal, obtain the lender’s actual decision rules, reason-mapping version, human assessment and delivered notice.

STUDY LIMITATIONS
The original score mechanism and authenticated provider reasons are absent.
The study uses historical repayment data and does not evaluate actual notices.
The analysis is exploratory and the forty records are not representative of lending decisions.
Interventional replacements can violate credit-feature relationships.
